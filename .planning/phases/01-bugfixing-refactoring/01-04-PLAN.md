## Decision recorded in this plan

**D-P04-01 — REF-04b surfaces per row, on the Game cell (resolves RESEARCH Open Question 1).**
REF-04's own wording is "Scan's status rows surface skipped unreadable paths", and an unreadable
subtree is a per-game fact. The aggregate alternative (one sweep-level StatusMessage warning) is
rejected: StatusMessage is the outcome surface Report() overwrites, and "progress and outcome
never share a surface" would make a sweep-level warning either flicker away or stomp a backup's
own result. The per-row surface needs only the body of `GameRowViewModel.ApplyStatus` — no
member is added, renamed or removed, so REQUIREMENTS.md's scope fence on GameRowViewModel (read
as "not a split or refactor target") and the prohibition on its public surface both hold; this
is the minimum touch REF-04's "status rows" wording requires. No XAML change: the Game cell's
existing StatusText line shows the sentence (with its existing ellipsis trimming), and the
backup's own outcome message names the same paths in full when the user backs up.
Rated reversible (one method body, one string key).
