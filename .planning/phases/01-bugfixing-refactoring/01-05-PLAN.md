## Decisions recorded in this plan

- **D-P05-01 — row-actions scope (resolves RESEARCH Open Question 2).** RowActionsCoordinator takes
  Back up, Restore and Open save folder: the three per-row flows that read `row.IsBusy` and a
  service answer and write no catalog. Change save folder and Remove game stay on MainViewModel:
  they are catalog-session gestures (writes through CatalogSession, then `Games` and selection
  mutations plus `RegisterRow`), and moving them would hand a coordinator the row lifecycle the
  view model owns. Rated reversible.
- **D-P05-02 — a host interface for this seam only.** The per-row flows interleave nine
  view-model touches (backup root, configured-root check, status line, report, rescan, and the
  two in-flight counters behind IsBackupRunning / IsRestoreRunning). Per-call delegates — the shape
  BulkBackupCoordinator uses for its two or three callbacks — would mean a nine-parameter
  signature, so this coordinator takes one internal `IRowActionHost`, implemented explicitly by
  MainViewModel (no public member added). It is the seam's own contract, not a new layer. Rated
  reversible (internal, one implementer).
