of MainViewModel into `BulkBackupCoordinator` (REF-02), preserving the claim/release order that
is the re-entrancy guard (REF-03), and close REF-04a: the 100-retry rename cap currently
surfaces as a generic disk error.

type, no observable state. MainViewModel keeps every bound property (BulkBackupTotal/Current/
CurrentGameName, the restore-sentence flag), resolves the backup root, constructs every
`Progress<T>` on the UI thread, rescans and reports. The coordinator writes rows and calls back
through `onStarted` / `onItem` / `onFinished` delegates for the view model's own state.

RunBulkBackupAsync / RestoreSelectedAsync wrappers, the REF-04a fix with two boundary facts, and

  edge text says "at exactly 100 colliding filenames … distinguishable message; at 99 collisions …
  succeeds". The shipped loop (BackupService.cs lines 152-169) tries the bare name (attempt 0) and

  moving that boundary, so the truths and tests pin the code's real boundary (101 vs 100 occupied
